About hereforanxiety

hereforanxiety is a private content-production and publishing service used by its account owner to create original narrated horror and psychological-thriller videos and upload approved videos to an authorised TikTok account.

The service combines original scripts, synthetic narration, captions and licensed or owned visual assets into short-form videos. It does not provide public user accounts, sell content, or ask visitors to sign in on this website.

How TikTok is used

After the account owner grants permission through TikTok Login Kit, hereforanxiety uses the Content Posting API only to upload videos selected by that owner. Access can be revoked at any time through TikTok's connected-app settings.

Contact

Questions about the service, privacy or account access may be submitted through the official project contact page.
