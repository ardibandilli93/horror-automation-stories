Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how hereforanxiety processes information when the account owner authorises the service to publish videos to TikTok.

Who this policy covers

hereforanxiety is a private content-production tool operated for its authorised account owner. The public website provides information only and does not offer public registration, profiles, comments, payments or advertising.

Information processed

  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens;
  • the connected account's TikTok identifier and basic profile information returned within approved scopes;
  • video files, titles, captions, hashtags and publishing choices;
  • upload status, error messages, workflow timestamps and identifiers used to prevent duplicate publishing.

hereforanxiety does not request or store a TikTok password.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is processed only to authenticate the account owner, create requested videos, upload authorised content, record publishing results, prevent duplicate uploads, maintain security and diagnose technical failures. Processing occurs at the account owner's request and with the consent granted through TikTok.

Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only as required with TikTok for authentication and publishing, and with GitHub for encrypted secrets, automated workflows, logs and temporary video artifacts. We do not sell personal information, create advertising profiles or share information with data brokers.

Storage and retention

Credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub repository secrets. Access tokens are retained only while the TikTok connection is active. Temporary rendered videos and workflow artifacts may be retained for up to 14 days. Technical logs may remain for the period provided by GitHub's configured retention settings. Publishing identifiers may be retained while necessary to prevent duplicate uploads.

Security

Reasonable safeguards include encrypted secrets, limited workflow access, HTTPS transport and restricted use of TikTok permissions. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

The connected account owner may revoke TikTok access at any time from TikTok's connected-app settings. Requests to access, correct or delete information may be submitted through the contact link below. When a valid deletion request is received, stored credentials and service-controlled records will be removed unless retention is required for security or legal reasons.

Cookies, children and international processing

This informational website does not intentionally set advertising cookies. hereforanxiety is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. GitHub and TikTok may process information in countries where they operate under their own privacy terms and safeguards.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated to reflect service or legal changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests may be submitted through the hereforanxiety project contact page.
