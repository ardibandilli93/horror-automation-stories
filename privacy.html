Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

This policy explains how Spare Key Stories handles information when an account owner connects TikTok and publishes a video.

Information processed

The service may process TikTok authorisation tokens, a TikTok account identifier, video files, captions, hashtags and technical status information required to upload and track a post. It does not request or store your TikTok password.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the connected account, render requested videos, upload them to TikTok, prevent duplicate uploads and diagnose failures.

Storage and sharing

Credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub repository secrets. Temporary video files and workflow logs may be retained by GitHub according to the repository's configured retention settings. Information is sent to TikTok only to perform authorised publishing. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Processing depends on GitHub and TikTok, which handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the service by revoking its access in TikTok and deleting the TikTok secrets from the GitHub repository. Workflow artifacts and logs can be deleted from GitHub. Tokens are retained only while needed for the authorised integration.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, including encrypted repository secrets and restricted token access. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the project contact page.
